Delete your account

Fogón Social account, used by Fabs, El Forzudo, La Cucha and La Alcancía · Español

Fabs and the other Fogón apps (El Forzudo, La Cucha and La Alcancía) share a single Fogón Social account. You can ask to delete it from any of them, and it is deleted for all of them. What you keep on your phone without an account is not touched: it stays with you.

From the app

  1. In Fabs: open Settings and, in the Account group, tap “Delete my network account”.
  2. In El Forzudo, La Cucha or La Alcancía: open Settings → Your network account and tap “Delete my network account”.
  3. If you like, tell us why you’re leaving (it’s optional and kept without anything that identifies you).
  4. Confirm with your password or, if you don’t have one, with the code sent to your email, and tap to delete.

Your account stops being visible immediately and your other sessions are closed. You have 7 days to change your mind: if you sign in before that date, you can keep it. After that, it is deleted.

Without the app

Write to fogonapps@gmail.com from your account’s email and ask us to delete it. If you no longer have access to that email, tell us your username and we’ll ask you how to prove the account is yours. We reply within 10 business days.

What is deleted

Everything of yours on the server: your profile (name, photo, cover, bio and links), your posts and reviews, comments, stories, notes, likes and saves, who you follow and who follows you, your messages, notifications, the shared lists you made, your votes, your sparks and what you own in the profile store, the photos and videos you uploaded, your sessions and the encrypted copy of your apps’ data.

What is kept and for how long

  • Your username stays reserved for 30 days, not linked to anything of yours, so nobody can pose as you right after you leave.
  • Reports you made are kept without your data, and resolved reports and moderation decisions are deleted after a year.
  • Content removed by moderation is kept hidden for up to 90 days and then deleted. If something of yours is under review because of a report, the deletion finishes when that review ends.
  • What the law requires us to keep for child safety is kept encrypted (no one on the team sees it in the app; only the person in charge can generate an encrypted copy to hand over to an authority that requests it in writing) and is deleted when they no longer need it.
  • The receipt of a purchase made on Google Play stays without your account, only so it can’t be used twice.

Delete some data without deleting the account

  • Posts and reviews: open their menu and tap “Delete”. You can also make them private: they are removed from the server.
  • Comments and stories: from their menu, “Delete”.
  • Your photo: remove it when editing your profile.
  • Your sessions: in Settings, “Where you are signed in”, close them.

To ask us to delete other data without deleting the account, write to fogonapps@gmail.com from your account’s email.
