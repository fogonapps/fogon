Child safety

Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation for Fabs and the other Fogón Social apps · Español

Our rule

Fabs and Fogón Social are only for people aged 18 or over. We prohibit any content or conduct that sexualizes, exploits or endangers anyone under 18: child sexual abuse material, grooming, sextortion, recruitment and trafficking. The rule applies to posts, comments, stories, profiles, the Market and messages.

How to report

  • In the app: open the menu of the post, comment, profile or Market item (or long-press the message in the chat), tap “Report” and choose “Sexual material involving minors”, the first reason on the list. For other risks to a minor, choose “It puts a minor at risk”.
  • By email: fogonapps@gmail.com, with the username or the link to what you saw. Do not attach or forward the material.
  • Do not take screenshots or forward the material, not even to report it: reporting it is enough.
  • If a child is in danger right now, call 123 (Police) or ICBF’s 141 line in Colombia, or the emergency services in your country.

What we do

  • The report goes to the top of the moderation queue and alerts the team right away. What was reported may be hidden while it is reviewed.
  • If it is confirmed, we remove the content, close the account that posted it and report it to the authorities of Colombia (National Police), with the data we have.
  • We keep the removed content encrypted: no one on the team sees it in the app; only the person in charge can generate an encrypted copy to hand over to an authority that requests it in writing.
  • We comply with Colombian law, in particular Law 679 of 2001 and Law 1336 of 2009.

Contact

Fogón’s child safety point of contact: fogonapps@gmail.com. We check it every day.
