1. What it is

Fogón Social is the optional part of the Fogón apps (El Cajón, El Forzudo and La Cucha) for sharing what you watch, read, listen to, train or cook and following other people; in El Cajón, also for messaging. One account works for all of them: sanctions, blocking and account deletion apply in all of them. It’s free, with no subscriptions or ads. Using it means accepting these terms and the privacy policy.

2. Who can use it

People aged 18 or over, with a single account of their own. You are responsible for what is done with your account.

3. What you post

What you post is yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to whoever you choose, only so the network works; that permission ends when you delete it or delete your account. Only post what you have the right to post.

4. What is not allowed

  • Harassing, threatening, discriminating or inciting violence.
  • Sexual content involving minors, explicit sexual content or graphic violence.
  • Posting other people’s personal data without their permission.
  • Fake or paid reviews, or creating places that don’t exist.
  • Impersonating someone, creating fake or mass accounts, spam or scams.
  • Uploading material that infringes copyright or trademarks.
  • Using programs to scrape data from the network or to attack it.

5. How we moderate

Fogón is small: one person reviews. We don’t watch everything that is posted; we act on reports. You can report accounts, posts, comments, messages and places, and block, mute or hide words whenever you want. What several people report may be hidden while we review it (whoever posted it still sees it, “under review”).

Strikes. If something breaks these terms, it is removed and counts as a strike (serious ones count double). Strikes expire after 90 days. With 1 active strike, a warning; with 2, 24 hours without posting, commenting or messaging; with 3, the account is suspended for 7 days; with 4, it is closed. It is closed at once for sexual content involving minors, real threats, being under 18 or opening an account to evade a sanction. We always tell you what was removed, which rule it broke and how long it lasts; your strikes are shown in your account.

Appeals. If you think we got it wrong, write to fogonapps@gmail.com within 30 days. We review it within a reasonable time; if we made a mistake, we restore what was removed and delete the strike.

Messages. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and we can’t read them: if you report one, you send us its text.

Minors. Sexual content involving minors is reported to the authorities of Colombia (and, where applicable, the United States), and we keep what is needed for the investigation.

Limits. To stop spam, new accounts have hourly limits on comments and messages during their first week. Your username can be changed once every 30 days.

The service is provided as is, with the care of a small project. It may fail, change or shut down; if it shuts down, we will give notice in advance. What’s in your apps always stays on your phone.
