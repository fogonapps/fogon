1. What it is

Fogón Social is the optional part of the Fogón apps (Fabs, Forz, Ñom and Pezi) for sharing what you watch, read, listen to, train or cook, or your saving achievements, and following other people; in Fabs, also for messaging. One account works for all of them: sanctions, blocking and account deletion apply in all of them. It’s free, with no subscriptions or ads. Using it means accepting these terms and the privacy policy.

2. Who can use it

People aged 18 or over, with a single account of their own. You are responsible for what is done with your account.

3. What you post

What you post is yours. You give us permission to store it and show it to whoever you choose, only so the network works; that permission ends when you delete it or delete your account. Only post what you have the right to post.

4. What is not allowed

  • Harassing, threatening, discriminating or inciting violence.
  • Any content or conduct that sexualizes, exploits or endangers anyone under 18: child sexual abuse material (including drawings or generated images), grooming, sextortion, recruitment or trafficking. See the child safety standards.
  • Explicit sexual content or graphic violence.
  • Posting other people’s personal data without their permission.
  • Fake or paid reviews, or creating places that don’t exist.
  • Impersonating someone, creating fake or mass accounts, spam or scams.
  • Uploading material that infringes copyright or trademarks.
  • Using programs to scrape data from the network or to attack it.

5. How we moderate

Fogón is small: one person reviews. We don’t watch everything that is posted; we act on reports. You can report accounts, posts, comments, messages and places, and block, mute or hide words whenever you want. What several people report may be hidden while we review it (whoever posted it still sees it, “under review”, and we don’t tell them who reported it); if we restore it, it comes back as it was. Also, a program on our own server checks whether the photos you upload look like nudity: if so, whatever uses them stays under review until a person on the team decides (no photo goes to third parties).

Strikes. If something breaks these terms, it is removed and counts as a strike (serious ones count double). Strikes expire after 90 days. With 1 active strike, a warning; with 2, 24 hours without posting, commenting or messaging; with 3, the account is suspended for 7 days; with 4, it is closed. It is closed at once for sexual content involving minors, real threats, being under 18 or opening an account to evade a sanction. When something is minor or a first time, we may give you just a warning: the post is removed but no strike is added. We always tell you in the app and by email what was removed, which rule it broke, how long it lasts and, when needed, a message from the team; your strikes are shown in your account.

Appeals. If you think we got it wrong, you can appeal from the app within 30 days (in Account status; if your account is suspended or closed, the app offers it when you sign in), once per sanction. A person on the team reviews it and replies by email; if we made a mistake, we remove the sanction and the strike. You can also write to fogonapps@gmail.com.

Messages. Messages are end-to-end encrypted and we can’t read them: if you report one, you send us its text. A new chat can only be started by the people you allow in “Who can message me” (by default, people who follow each other with you); the chats you already have stay as they are. For the 30 days after this change, if you kept the default option, the previous rule still applies between accounts created before the change, and every chat with a message in that time becomes a chat you already have; if you chose “No one” or “People I follow”, it applies right away, and accounts created after the change use the new rule. After the 30 days, only the chats you already have stay as they are.

Minors. Sexual content involving minors is reported with the reason “Sexual material involving minors”, reported to the authorities of Colombia and, where applicable, the United States, and kept encrypted: no one on the team sees it in the app; only the person in charge can generate an encrypted copy to hand over to an authority that requests it in writing. The account that posted it is closed. More in the child safety standards.

Limits. To stop spam, new accounts have hourly limits on comments and messages during their first week. Your username can be changed once every 30 days.

5b. Sparks and the profile store

Sparks are earned by using the apps (a welcome amount and some each day you open them) and can also be bought in the app: with Wompi or, in apps downloaded from Google Play, with Google Play. They are spent on profile backgrounds and frames in the store and in the Market, where whoever creates an item receives part of the sparks from each sale. They can’t be exchanged for money or sent to other accounts (you can gift a Market item), and there are no random draws or loot boxes: what you choose is what you get. If a Google Play purchase is refunded or voided, the sparks from that purchase are deducted; if they were already spent, the rest becomes a pending balance and you can’t spend sparks until it is covered. Purchase claims are handled at fogonapps@gmail.com. If the account is closed, they are lost. We may change store prices or items; what you already have stays yours.

The service is provided as is, with the care of a small project. It may fail, change or shut down; if it shuts down, we will give notice in advance. What’s in your apps always stays on your phone.
