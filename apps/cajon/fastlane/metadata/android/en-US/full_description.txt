<p><b>Beta:</b> El Cajón may still change and have bugs. Known issues are listed at <a href="https://github.com/fogonapps/el-cajon#problemas-conocidos">github.com/fogonapps/el-cajon</a>.</p>
<p>El Cajón keeps everything you watch, read and listen to: films, series, anime, books, video games, albums and artists, in one place, with your rating and your diary. No ads. We don’t sell your data. It’s part of <b>Fogón</b>, everyday apps with no subscriptions: yours from day one.</p>
<p><b>The network (optional):</b> create your @username, follow people, see what they share on Home and post your reviews, photos and games in your stories, with likes, comments and saves. Only what you mark as public or for your followers is uploaded.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Everything in one cajón:</b> films, series, anime, books, records and video games, with cover (change it or upload your own), year, director or author, genres and synopsis.</li>
<li><b>Search and add:</b> books from Open Library and records and artists from MusicBrainz, no key needed; films, series and anime from TMDB with your own free key. Or add them by hand.</li>
<li><b>Video games:</b> search for a game (cover, screenshots, critic and player ratings, summary and links, from IGDB) and mark it playing, finished, want to play or dropped, with a rating and review. They have their own tab, Games.</li>
<li><b>Star ratings</b> (in halves), likes, reviews, trivia, tags for everything and favorite episodes.</li>
<li><b>Diary:</b> every time you watch, read or listen to something, with date, rating and whether it was a rewatch.</li>
<li><b>To watch, to read, to listen:</b> what’s up next, what you’re in the middle of and what you finished.</li>
<li><b>Public ratings</b> next to yours: TMDB, Open Library or MusicBrainz.</li>
<li><b>Progress:</b> pages for books and every album track with its own rating and heart.</li>
<li><b>Film, Music, Books and Games</b>, each with its own section, and a Home with everything you’ve watched, listened to, read and played.</li>
<li><b>Artists, writers and directors</b> with their photo and their records, books or films, to add them with one tap.</li>
<li><b>Your own lists</b>, ranked if you like, and your top four on your profile, with your favorite game.</li>
<li><b>Your stats:</b> what you finished this year, hours of film and series, pages, tracks, games, your ratings, months, genres and creators. Plus yearly challenges.</li>
<li><b>Share</b> a review or a list as an image.</li>
<li><b>Bring what you had:</b> import from Letterboxd (ZIP or CSV), Netflix (viewing activity CSV), Trakt (ZIP or JSON), TV Time (ZIP or CSV), Goodreads and The StoryGraph (CSV). Nothing gets duplicated.</li>
<li><b>Encrypted copy:</b> if you use a Fogón account, you can keep an encrypted copy of your collection.</li>
<li><b>In Spanish and English.</b></li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your data is yours.</b> Everything stays on your phone. Internet is only used to search, fetch covers and, if you turn it on, sync. Save a backup any time.</p>

<p>El Cajón uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. Video game data and images from IGDB (igdb.com); games not on IGDB come from Wikidata (CC0), with covers from Wikimedia Commons credited to their author and license.</p>
