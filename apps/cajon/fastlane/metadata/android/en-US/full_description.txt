<p>El Cajón keeps everything you watch, read and listen to: films, series, anime, books, albums and artists, in one place, with your rating and your diary. No ads, no tracking. It’s part of <b>Fogón</b>, home-made apps: your attention is worth more than an ad.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Everything in one cajón:</b> films, series, anime, books and records, with cover, year, director or author, genres and synopsis.</li>
<li><b>Search and add:</b> books from Open Library and records and artists from MusicBrainz, no key needed; films, series and anime from TMDB with your own free key. Or add them by hand.</li>
<li><b>Star ratings</b> (in halves), likes, reviews, trivia and tags.</li>
<li><b>Diary:</b> every time you watch, read or listen to something, with date, rating and whether it was a rewatch.</li>
<li><b>To watch, to read, to listen:</b> what’s up next, what you’re in the middle of and what you finished.</li>
<li><b>Public ratings</b> next to yours: TMDB, Open Library or MusicBrainz.</li>
<li><b>Progress:</b> pages for books and every album track with its own rating and heart.</li>
<li><b>Film, Music and Books</b>, each with its own section, and a Home with everything you’ve watched, listened to and read.</li>
<li><b>Artists, writers and directors</b> with their photo and their records, books or films, to add them with one tap.</li>
<li><b>Your own lists</b>, ranked if you like, and your top four on your profile.</li>
<li><b>Your stats:</b> what you finished this year, hours of film and series, pages, tracks, your ratings, months, genres and creators. Plus yearly challenges.</li>
<li><b>Share</b> a review or a list as an image.</li>
<li><b>Bring what you had:</b> import your Letterboxd history (ZIP or CSV) and your Goodreads library (CSV).</li>
<li><b>Phone and PC:</b> if you like, it syncs through a secret file in your GitHub account.</li>
<li><b>In Spanish and English.</b></li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your data is yours.</b> Everything stays on your phone. Internet is only used to search, fetch covers and, if you turn it on, sync. Save a backup any time.</p>

<p>El Cajón uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.</p>
