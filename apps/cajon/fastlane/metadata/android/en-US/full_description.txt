<p>Fabs is your diary of what you watch, read, listen to and play: films, series, anime, books, records, artists and video games, in one place, with your rating and your dates. No ads. We don’t sell your data. It’s part of <b>Fogón</b>: everyday apps with no subscriptions.</p>
<p><b>What’s new</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Log in two taps:</b> add your rating and a like and, if you finished it today, it lands in your diary.</li>
<li><b>Diary as a grid:</b> your covers by month, with the rating from each time. Prefer a list? That works too.</li>
<li><b>Year in review:</b> what you finished, your top rated, your busiest month and your streak, ready to share as an image.</li>
<li><b>Bring what you already logged:</b> import from Letterboxd, Goodreads, The StoryGraph, Trakt, TV Time, Netflix and Steam. Nothing gets duplicated.</li>
<li><b>Together:</b> with someone you follow back, see what to watch with them: what you both have on your lists, where each of you is up to, and what you both finished.</li>
<li><b>Community reviews:</b> in People, read what others write, even without an account.</li>
<li><b>Profile store (optional):</b> banners, frames and backgrounds for your profile. They’re bought with sparks (chispas), which you purchase inside the app. These are optional purchases: everything else works without them.</li>
</ul>
<p><b>The essentials</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Everything in one place:</b> films, series, anime, books, records and video games, with cover (change it or upload your own), year, creator, genres and synopsis.</li>
<li><b>Search and add:</b> books from Open Library, records and artists from MusicBrainz, films, series and anime from TMDB, games from IGDB. Or add them by hand.</li>
<li><b>Star ratings</b> (in halves), likes, reviews, tags and the public rating next to yours.</li>
<li><b>To watch, to read, to listen:</b> what’s up next, what you’re in the middle of and what you finished.</li>
<li><b>Your own lists</b> and your top four on your profile.</li>
<li><b>Your stats:</b> what you finished this year, hours, pages, tracks, genres and creators, plus yearly challenges.</li>
<li><b>Optional network:</b> create your @username, follow people and post your reviews. Only what you mark as public or for your followers is uploaded.</li>
<li><b>Encrypted copy</b> of your collection with your Fogón account, if you want it.</li>
<li><b>In Spanish and English.</b></li>
</ul>
<p><b>Your data is yours.</b> Everything stays on your phone. Internet is only used to search, fetch covers and, if you turn them on, the network and the encrypted copy. You can download a copy of your data any time.</p>
<p>Fabs uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. Video game data and images from IGDB (igdb.com); games not on IGDB come from Wikidata (CC0), with covers from Wikimedia Commons credited to their author and license.</p>
