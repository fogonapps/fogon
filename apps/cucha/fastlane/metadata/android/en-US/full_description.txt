<p>La Cucha helps you keep track of what you eat: calories, protein, your recipes and the weekly menu. No ads, no tracking, and no internet except to look up a barcode or for your people, if you turn them on. It’s part of <b>Fogón</b>, everyday apps with no subscriptions: yours from day one.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Today:</b> the calories you have left, protein, carbs and fat, water and the day’s five meals.</li>
<li><b>397 foods, Colombian ones included</b> (arepa, beans, patacón, guava paste, panela, mushrooms, cheese bread…) with home portions: a large egg, a medium arepa, a cup. Search works with or without accents or plurals.</li>
<li><b>Build your plate:</b> add ingredients one after another and save it as a recipe to repeat it with one tap.</li>
<li><b>Values from USDA FoodData Central,</b> and every food says where they come from.</li>
<li><b>Optional barcode lookup:</b> scan the package and find the product on Open Food Facts, or type in the label yourself.</li>
<li><b>129 recipes</b> (ajiaco, sancocho, bandeja paisa, mondongo, bean casserole, changua, eggs with mushrooms…) with calories per serving. Create your own or import them.</li>
<li><b>Weekly menu</b>, with alternating weeks A and B if you like, and a <b>shopping list</b> built from it.</li>
<li><b>Goals</b> calculated from your details, or set by hand if you follow a dietitian’s plan.</li>
<li><b>Progress:</b> streak, weekly average, the last 14 days and your weight curve.</li>
<li><b>Linked with El Forzudo:</b> it reads your weight and workouts from there, inside your phone, from your own profile.</li>
<li><b>Your people, optional:</b> with a Fogón Social account you share dishes, recipes and restaurant reviews and follow other people. Calories only go out if you include them; a restaurant’s location only if you tap “Use my location” (permission is requested at that moment).</li>
<li><b>Widget</b> with what’s left for today and a button to log food.</li>
<li><b>In Spanish and English.</b></li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your data is yours.</b> Everything stays on your phone. Only a barcode number leaves it, if you turn that option on, and what you share with your people. Your diary, goals and weight never leave it. Save a backup any time.</p>

<p>La Cucha isn’t medical or nutrition advice. Food values are estimates; if you have a health condition, follow your doctor’s or dietitian’s advice.</p>
