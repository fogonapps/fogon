New look: everything is printed like a riso, with two inks and the second one offset. Chunky two-ink icons and categories, halftone dots instead of gradients and the main card as a block of ink. Appearance: five styles (Night, Riso, Rebel, Bubblegum and Contrast); in each one you pick the paper, the inks, how far the ink is offset and the decoration, plus every colour. Colours you had changed go back to the style defaults.
