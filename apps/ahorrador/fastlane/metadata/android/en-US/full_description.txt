<p>La Alcancía helps you see where your money goes and save for what you want. No ads, no tracking and no bank passwords. It’s part of <b>Fogón</b>, everyday apps with no subscriptions: yours from day one.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Log in seconds</b> with a big keypad: expenses, income, transfers between accounts and savings.</li>
<li><b>Accounts:</b> cash, digital wallet, bank and credit card.</li>
<li><b>Credit card:</b> what you owe, limit, statement and due dates, instalment purchases, and an estimate of the minimum payment and interest.</li>
<li><b>Four ways to budget:</b> envelopes, the 50/30/20 rule, kakeibo (with a month wrap-up) or just tracking.</li>
<li><b>Savings goals:</b> how much to set aside every two weeks to make it on time.</li>
<li><b>Fixed payments</b> with a reminder before they’re due, logged automatically if you like.</li>
<li><b>Owed to me and I owe,</b> with partial payments.</li>
<li><b>Colombia’s 4×1000 tax</b> logged automatically, optional.</li>
<li><b>Lock</b> with your fingerprint or phone lock.</li>
<li><b>Log several</b> by typing them (“lunch 25k + uber 12k”) and <b>“Can I afford it?”</b> before spending, with your daily allowance. Everything is calculated on your phone.</li>
<li><b>Your people, optional:</b> with a Fogón Social account you share your saving achievements and your goals as a percentage. Never amounts, accounts or transactions.</li>
<li><b>In Spanish and English.</b></li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your data is yours.</b> Everything stays on your phone. Without a Fogón Social account the app connects to nothing; with one, only the achievements you share go out. Save a backup any time and export your entries to a spreadsheet.</p>

<p>La Alcancía isn’t financial advice. Card minimum payments and interest are estimates: the real figures are on your bank statement.</p>

<p><b>Record from your bank (optional):</b> recognizes Bancolombia payments and transfers delivered by SMS and outgoing transfers from Nu. Link your accounts and confirm before recording, or enable automatic recording per bank to review and undo later. Detects transfers between your accounts.</p>
<p><b>Notification access:</b> Android lets this access see all notifications. La Alcancía filters by app and selected bank first: only SMS starting with “Bancolombia:” and recognized outgoing transfers from the Nu app are processed. Everything else is never stored. SMS history is never read. Turn it off in Profile or revoke access in Android. Nothing leaves your phone.</p>
